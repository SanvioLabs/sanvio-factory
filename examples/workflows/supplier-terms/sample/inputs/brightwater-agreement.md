# Supply agreement: Brightwater Dairy and Juniper Bakery

Invented for this example. Brightwater Dairy, Juniper Bakery and this agreement
are made up.

## 1. Parties

1.1 Brightwater Dairy ("the supplier") supplies butter and cream to Juniper Bakery ("the bakery").

## 2. Term

2.1 This agreement starts on ________ and runs for twelve months.

## 3. Payment

3.1 The bakery pays each invoice within 45 days of receiving it.

## 4. Prices

4.1 The prices are:

| Item | Price |
|---|---|
| Unsalted butter, 10 kg block | $84.00 |
| Double cream, 5 litre tub | $21.50 |

4.2 Prices rise by no more than 3% a year, on each anniversary of the start date.

## 5. Delivery

5.1 Deliveries come on Tuesday and Friday, before 6:30am.

5.2 The minimum order per delivery is 60 kg of unsalted butter.

## 6. General

6.1 Each party sends notices to the other in writing.

6.2 Neither party is liable for a delay caused by events outside its control.

6.3 This agreement continues for a further twelve months unless the bakery gives 90 days' written notice before it ends, and renews on the same terms each year after that.
