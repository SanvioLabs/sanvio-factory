# Juniper Bakery purchasing rules (sample)

Invented for this example. A sample standard: replace these rules with your
own, and leave the copy in sample/standard/ as it is.

S1. Payment terms are 30 days or more.
S2. Deliveries come at least twice a week, before 7am.
S3. Every price in the supplier's price list matches the price the agreement gives for that item.
S4. Any price rise is capped at 5% a year, and the agreement says so.
S5. No minimum order per delivery is above 40 kg of any one item.
S6. The agreement says when it starts and how long it runs.
S7. The agreement doesn't renew automatically. Search all of it for "renew", not only the sections whose headings look like they matter.
