# Supplier terms

Invented for this example. Juniper Bakery, its suppliers and its people are
made up.

## The last real time

Maya, in her own words:

"Hollow Oak Mills sent their renewal on a Tuesday. I saved the PDF and their
price list into the Suppliers folder, then opened our purchasing rules next to
it. I went rule by rule. Payment terms had to be 30 days or more, and they were
30. Delivery had to be twice a week before 7am, and it was. The price rise had
to be capped at 5% a year, and theirs said 'in line with market conditions', so
I marked that as not meeting rule 4. I wrote it up on our one-page form, put
the clause numbers next to each line, and left it on Tom's desk. He asked them
to put a cap in, and they did."

## The time it went sideways

"Last spring Brightwater Dairy's agreement renewed itself. The renewal clause
was on page 9, under 'General', and I'd only read the sections with headings
that looked like they mattered. We were locked in for another year at the old
minimum order, which was more butter than we use outside the holidays. I only
noticed when the invoice came. Since then rule 7 says to look for any automatic
renewal, wherever it is, and I search the whole PDF for 'renew'."
